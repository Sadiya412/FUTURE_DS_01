# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Future Interns Task 01

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the Data Science & Analytics Internship Track by Future Interns (Task 01).

The objective of this project was to analyze e-commerce sales data and build an interactive business intelligence dashboard that provides insights into:

* sales performance
* profitability
* customer behavior
* regional trends
* product performance

The dashboard was built using Microsoft Power BI to support data-driven business decision-making.

---

# Business Problem

Businesses generate large amounts of sales data, but raw data alone does not provide meaningful insights.

This project aims to transform raw e-commerce data into clear visual insights that can help stakeholders:

* improve operational efficiency
* support strategic business decisions

# Key Insights

## 1. Technology Category Generated the Highest Profit

Technology products contributed the largest share of total profit, making it the strongest-performing category.

### Recommendation

Increase marketing and inventory investment for technology products.

---

## 2. West Region Achieved the Highest Sales

The West region outperformed all other regions in revenue generation.

### Recommendation

Expand regional campaigns and customer retention strategies in high-performing areas.

---

## 3. Consumer Segment Dominated Sales

The Consumer segment contributed the majority of total sales.

### Recommendation

Focus on personalized promotions and loyalty strategies for consumer customers.

---

## 4. Furniture Category Had Lower Profitability

Despite high sales volume, the Furniture category generated relatively lower profit margins.

### Recommendation

Review pricing strategies, discounts, and shipping costs for furniture products.

---

## 5. Standard Class Was the Most Used Shipping Mode

Most customers preferred Standard Class shipping.

### Recommendation

Optimize logistics efficiency and delivery operations for standard shipping services.
